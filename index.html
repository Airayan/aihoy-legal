No boat needed. Just an idea and a date.

A private boat is lovely — it just doesn't add up for two. And on the big boat you're one of forty, in the smoke, with no shade.

Aihoy! is the missing step between the two: it shows you who'll be there at the same time — and once six of you get together, the private boat costs the same per head as the crowded one.

Just launched — be patient

In the first weeks the radar will be quiet. That's not a bug, it's the beginning. An app like this is like a new bar: it gets good once people start showing up.

If you like what you see: tell one person heading to the water this year. That's enough.

An old idea, a quarter century late

Twenty-five years ago I built a hotel-booking system. It worked. I just never saw how big the opportunity was — years later it hit me that I'd once had Booking.com in my hands, and hadn't noticed.

It doesn't weigh on me. I've made peace with the fact that back then I couldn't see it — it was meant to be. But now that there's a second idea in front of me, I don't want to let go until I get to the point where it no longer depends on me alone.

There's one thing I want to find out: whether it works.

I build Aihoy! alone, in the evenings, alongside work and a small child. No company, no investor, no marketing budget behind it — just one person looking for an answer.
