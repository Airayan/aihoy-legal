Child Safety

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 2026 · Aihoy! — The Vibematcher

Age Restriction

Aihoy! is strictly an 18+ platform. The app is designed exclusively for adults. Users under the age of 18 are not permitted to register or use the service.

Age verification measures are in place during registration, and accounts found to belong to minors are immediately suspended and deleted.

Zero Tolerance for CSAE

Aihoy! has a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to preventing, detecting, and responding to any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors.

  • No content involving minors in a sexual context is permitted under any circumstances
  • Any user found sharing, soliciting, or distributing CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material) is immediately banned and reported to authorities
  • All user-generated content is subject to review and moderation
  • Suspicious accounts targeting minors are proactively removed

We report all confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies.

In-App Reporting

Aihoy! provides users with accessible in-app tools to report any content or behavior that raises child safety concerns.

  • Every user profile has a Report button accessible from the profile view
  • Every chat conversation has a report option
  • Reports are reviewed promptly by our team
  • Reported accounts are investigated and actioned within 24 hours for critical safety issues

Moderation & Prevention

We take proactive steps to prevent child exploitation on our platform:

  • Registration requires date of birth confirmation — users under 18 cannot create accounts
  • Profile photos are subject to content moderation
  • Automated systems flag suspicious content and behavior patterns
  • User accounts are reviewed when reported for safety violations
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations involving minors

Legal Compliance

Aihoy! complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • EU Digital Services Act (DSA) child protection provisions
  • GDPR special protections for minors
  • Local laws in all jurisdictions where the app is available

We are committed to staying current with evolving child safety regulations and updating our practices accordingly.
