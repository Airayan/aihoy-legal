Average speed helper for cars and motorbikes

Keep the average you set.

Set a target average and a distance. Averagers shows the average you’ll end up with if you carry on like this — green when you’re under, red when you’d go over.

Free · No account · No ads · 11 languages

Shown with the Hungarian interface — the app speaks your phone’s language (11 languages).

What it does

Projected average

At a glance: the average you’ll have at the end of the distance if you keep going like this.

Green or red

Green while you’re under your target, red when you’d go over. Readable from the mount.

Sound and vibration

A beep when it turns red, a vibration if you keep going faster than your target. Both can be switched off.

Starts by itself

The timer starts when you actually start moving, not when you press the button.

Works in the background

Keeps measuring with the screen off, next to your navigation or music.

Community suggestions

Where several drivers measured, start with the most common setting in one tap.

Pro feature · free during testing · Android

Floating window over your navigation

Switch to your map or navigation app while measuring, and a small window keeps showing the projected average and the highest speed you can still keep. Drag it anywhere, set its size and transparency, tap it to come back. The background turns green or red just like in the app.

Privacy first

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location is processed on your phone. For community suggestions only rounded start and end points and the target you chose are shared anonymously — you can switch it off and delete it any time.

Important

Averagers is a tool for calculating average speed — it is not meant to help anyone break traffic rules. Always follow the traffic rules and drive according to road conditions; road signs and speed limits always take priority. GPS-based data is for information only. Set up the app before you drive and don’t use your phone while driving.
